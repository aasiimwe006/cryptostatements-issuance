Issuance archive

Mint, burn, freeze, wipe and pause events from the USDC, USDT and PAXG contracts on Ethereum, read from the chain once a day. What each contract recorded, with block, time and transaction; never why.

index.json holds the daily totals. Each day's events are a file in that month's release, and each complete month is also one compressed file there.

USDC mints and burns on Ethereum include Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol, which burns on one chain and mints on another: they show the supply on Ethereum, not issuance or redemption of the whole token.
